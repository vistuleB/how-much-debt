## When does the bond floor bind?

“Money versus government debt” is not a menu-independent dichotomy. In the narrow restrictive experiments, bonds are frozen, so K=B precisely when no selected loans remain. This does not assert K equals nonbank money otherwise.

- Headline three-operation pooled F2: bond floor binds in 2019, 2020, 2023, 2024, 2025; later departures after the first equality: 2021, 2022. All-completion counts above prevent treating the headline as universal.
- Headline five-operation pooled F2: bond floor binds in 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025; later departures after the first equality: 2018. All-completion counts above prevent treating the headline as universal.

The broad table allows redemption and changes what counts as debt, so its Treasury floor is a different quantity. Its domestic gap must not be substituted into a narrower model or household slicing without matching the instrument and permission definitions.
